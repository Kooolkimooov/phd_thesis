\section{Unmanned underwater robots}

This section provides an overview of unmanned underwater robots, which are essential for various
applications such as marine research, environmental monitoring, and underwater exploration. These
robots can generally be classified into two main categories based on their operational modes:

\begin{itemize}
	\item Remotely operated vehicles use a tether to connect to a surface vessel, allowing for real-time
	      feedback, control, teleoperation, and power supply.
	\item Autonomous underwater vehicles referred to as ``AUVs'' operate independently, relying on
	      pre-programmed missions and onboard sensors for navigation and data collection.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Remotely operated vehicles}

Remotely operated vehicles referred to as ``ROVs'' use a tether to connect to a surface vessel and
are typically equipped with cameras, sensors, and manipulators for various underwater tasks. The
use of tethers is necessitated by the absorption of electromagnetic waves in water which prevents
wireless communications at a range greater than a few meters.
